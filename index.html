Gangni Gonit Poribar, a community math club in Gangni, Meherpur

Since 2012, we have been helping school students in our rural community enjoy mathematics, think clearly, and solve problems with confidence.

A family built around mathematics

“Gonit Poribar” means “math family”. Since 2012 the club has brought school students in Gangni together to learn mathematics beyond the classroom, and we still run regular classes for students in the area.

What we teach

Our classes go beyond the textbook. Students learn to reason, ask questions and enjoy hard problems.

ℕ

Number theory

Primes, divisibility and patterns in numbers, the heart of math olympiad problems.

P(A)

Probability

Counting, chance and games, learned through puzzles and simple experiments.

x̄

Statistics

Reading data, averages and charts, so students can make sense of numbers around them.

{ }

Programming

First steps in coding and logical thinking, turning math ideas into working programs.

Our story

  1. 2012

    Gangni Gonit Poribar is founded in Gangni, Meherpur, to give local school students a place to learn mathematics together.

  2. 2013

    The club is recognised as Best Math Club at the Bangladesh Mathematical Olympiad.

  3. Today

    Classes continue, now covering number theory, probability, statistics and programming. New sessions are announced on our Facebook page.
